# Community Age / Minors Product Decision — 2026-10-02

Status: **CLOSED / PRODUCT APPROVED — PRIVACY + LEGAL REVIEW STILL REQUIRED**

## Owner-approved Product policy

- The minimum age for an **independent Wand Account is 13**.
- Users **under 13 may not independently register a Wand Account**.
- This is an **account-registration boundary**, not a ban on using Dreamwish Wand.
- Accountless/local functionality remains available, including local save-file editing and local Editor/read-only workflows.
- A parent or guardian may use their own Wand Account to create/manage a `parent_guardian_managed` DDV Profile Workspace for an under-13 user's game profile.
- The under-13 user does **not** receive separate Wand credentials.
- A parent/guardian-managed Workspace uses the same **maximum five retained Profile Workspaces per Wand Account** as self-managed Workspaces. Active and Archived Workspaces both count. DDV Player ID/mdc association is optional.

## Explicit Product boundary

This decision does **not** authorize or select:

- an age-verification/age-assurance provider;
- collection of a full date of birth when a less intrusive gate can satisfy the final requirements;
- a complex parental-consent implementation;
- independent under-13 accounts.

Those implementation choices remain subject to Privacy/Legal review.

## Under-13 parent-managed Community participation

Product approves the **parent/guardian-managed account association model**.

The exact permitted scope for an under-13 user's Community participation through that account remains a targeted external Legal/Privacy question. In particular, external review must determine the required conditions for:

- publishing Gallery / Preset / DreamSnaps content;
- comments and replies;
- Q&A / Tips;
- follows, reactions and saves;
- public Creator attribution and other public profile surfaces.

Until that review is resolved, engineering must not infer that parent-managed Workspace automatically authorizes every Community interaction for an under-13 user.

## Ages 13–17

Users aged 13 or older are Product-eligible for their own Wand Account. Final Privacy/Legal review must still define any minor-specific defaults, notices, safety controls or consent requirements that apply to users aged 13–17 in launch jurisdictions.
