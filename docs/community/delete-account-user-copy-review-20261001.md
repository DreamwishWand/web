## Short privacy-policy section

### Account deletion and data retention

When you delete your Wand Account, access to the account ends immediately. Your Creator Profile and
works are removed from Community access and discovery, your comments are anonymized, and private
interaction data such as saves, follows, reactions, notifications, and linked DDV profiles is
removed.

User-authored content payload, including media and Preset data, is normally physically purged
within 7 days. This period is for secure asynchronous deletion and retry handling and is not an
account-recovery period.

Operational, security, and moderation records are normally deleted or minimized within 90 days.
Specific information may be retained longer when a documented moderation, security, or legal hold
is required. The hold must be limited to the period for which that need continues.

Wand may preserve minimal non-content technical records where necessary to maintain the integrity
of revision, moderation, reporting, and audit relationships. These records must not be used as a
reason to retain deleted user-authored content or unnecessary identifying information.

Database backups, Storage recovery copies, and copies or logs held by infrastructure,
authentication, or transactional-email providers follow separate recovery or provider retention
periods. Wand minimizes the data sent to those providers. Restored Community data is quarantined
and deletion records are reapplied before a recovered environment may become user-visible.

The final Privacy Policy must identify the production service providers and material
provider-controlled retention periods in effect at launch.
